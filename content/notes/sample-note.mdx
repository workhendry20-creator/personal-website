## Why a Narrative Personal Space?

Traditional portfolios often feel like walking CVs—showcasing lists of tech stacks, projects without context, and overly formal tone.

Through this personal space, the primary focus shifts to narrative: how ideas are formed, learning at the intersection of Information Systems and Physics, and driving active youth diplomacy.

### Core Pillars
- **Typography-first:** Prioritizing reading comfort and clarity.
- **Thought Garden:** Treating notes as evolving reflections over time.
