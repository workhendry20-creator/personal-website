## Overview

Kandang Kang Iky is a modern, responsive landing page engineered to elevate the digital presence, product showcase, and customer engagement for a local livestock enterprise.

## Features

- **Product Catalog Showcase:** Interactive display of livestock categories, pricing, and health certifications.
- **Direct Order Integration:** Seamless WhatsApp and contact integration for direct customer inquiries.
- **Responsive Editorial Design:** Mobile-optimized layout with fast loading times and clean aesthetics.

## Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
