## Overview

SITKA (Sistem Informasi Tumbuh Kembang Anak) Frontend is an integrated multi-role dashboard platform designed for early childhood education management at Paud SPS Flamboyan.

## Features

- **Multi-Role Access:** Customized portal interfaces for school administration, teachers, and parents.
- **Student Growth Analytics:** Visual tracking of physical, cognitive, and social development milestones.
- **Academic & Attendance Records:** Streamlined grading, attendance logging, and report card generation.

## Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js (App Router)
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
